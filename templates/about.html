About Perk Miner

Pay-per-sale advertising built around consumer privacy, cash rewards and word-of-mouth.
For our members
  • Always free. No membership or subscription fees, no contracts, no commitment.
  • Privacy first. We only collect your name and email to create your account. We never sell, share or harvest your data as a lead.
  • One business at a time. You connect through secure messaging and decide who gets your contact info, and when. No spam, no unsolicited calls, no uninvited salespeople.
  • Real cash rewards. 2% cash back on up to $2,500 of each purchase, plus referral commissions when people and businesses you refer make qualifying purchases or sales.
  • Word of mouth pays. Up to 6 members (when all payouts are active) earn up to 56% of the ad revenue from every finalized transaction.
  • Live GPS tracking. For service calls, see the provider's status, their exact location and ETA once "on the way", and confirm or correct the destination address.
For our advertisers
  • Zero wasted ad dollars. True Pay-Per-Sale / Pay-Per-Close: no sale or closed deal means no fee.
  • Predictable, sale-based cost. Pay 10% of the sale, capped at $250 — about $1 in fees per $10 sold, before your own costs. No hidden fees, contracts or subscriptions.
  • Exclusive leads, free. One member to one business at a time. Leads, PPC, SEO, website and foot traffic, calls, appointments and meet-ups all cost nothing.
  • Real cash rewards. 1% cash back on up to $2,500 of every finalized sale (for offering at least one perk to members), plus referral commissions on businesses you refer.
  • Word of mouth pays. Up to 6 advertisers (when all payouts are active) earn up to 28% of the ad revenue from every finalized transaction.
  • Free listing, low start-up. Start with as little as $25 in pre-funded ad dollars (not a fee). Unused funds stay in your balance, and the ad fee is deducted only when a sale is finalized.
  • Live GPS tracking. Real-time status and location of your field team when "on the way", and members can correct a wrong address before anyone drives there.
Everyone wins. No gimmicks, no fees to join, no contracts. All cash back and commissions are paid by Perk Miner LLC from the ad revenue received, and it is real cash you can withdraw to your bank (no points or gift cards).
