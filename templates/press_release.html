Perk Miner Launches Pay-Per-Sale Advertising Platform Built Around Consumer Privacy

Businesses pay only when a sale closes, while members control when their contact information is shared.

ContactJoe Mendez
TitleFounder, Perk Miner LLC
Emailsupport@perkminer.com
WebsitePerkMiner.com

LAS VEGAS, NV — October 12, 2026 — Perk Miner today announced the launch of its privacy-first advertising and rewards platform, designed to connect businesses and members through secure messaging without ever compromising member personal data. Advertising fees are tied to completed sales rather than impressions, clicks, leads, calls, or appointments.

How it works

Advertisers maintain a pre-funded working balance to cover the advertising fees and funds are drawn only when a qualifying transaction is completed. There are no contracts, subscriptions, or hidden fees.

Members pay businesses directly for the purchase of all products or for services rendered and upon successful finalization, a 10% advertising fee, capped at $250 is deducted from the advertiser’s account balance.

This represents a minimum 10x revenue-to-advertising-cost ratio, or 1,000% return on ad spend (ROAS), before cost of goods sold.

Our model is designed to help our advertisers increase their profit margins, while providing a safe, privacy-focused marketplace for our members. Businesses get zero wasted advertising dollars and our members never have to worry about receiving unsolicited phone calls, spam emails or uninvited sales people showing up at their door when searching for a business, product or service.

— Joe Mendez, Founder of Perk Miner LLC

Privacy first

🔒Unlike other marketing agencies that collect consumer data by tracking browsing history, using call to action forms on their website or utilizing technology that listens to conversations, Perk Miner LLC will never utilize any of these data collection techniques nor collect and store member information, except for a name and email for account creation purposes.

Even more importantly, members never have to worry about their data being sold or them being exploited for monetization purposes, which is a common practice used by many companies.

Real cash rewards

Perk Miner LLC re-invests up to 84% of its advertising revenue from every finalized transaction back into the ecosystem with its rewards program in the form of withdrawable earnings deposited to bank accounts; not points, coupons or gift cards.

2% For members

Members receive 2% cash back on qualifying purchases (up to $2,500 of every purchase).

Members can earn referral commissions when people and businesses they refer generate qualifying purchases or sales.

1% For advertisers

Advertisers receive 1% cash back on up to $2,500 of every qualified sale in exchange for offering a perk to members.

Businesses can earn referral commissions when merchants they introduce to the ecosystem generate qualifying sales.

Both sides can earn additional revenue through referrals.

All cashback and referral commissions are paid by Perk Miner LLC and not from its advertisers.

Live tracking for service jobs

For service jobs, live en-route location of service providers and address confirmation are included to help reduce wrong-address trips and to keep members and business owners updated with live status updates at no cost to advertisers.
