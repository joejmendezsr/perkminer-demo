What to say if their smallest sale is more than $10

If the smallest sale they usually make is more than $10, they add funds to their account balance to cover the advertising fee. Be clear that this is not an advertising fee. It is pre-funded advertising dollars that stay in their account balance until they make a sale or close a deal. Fees are taken from that balance only when a sale is finalized, and unused money stays in their balance.

Tip: mention the code in the first message. "Zero up-front cost" is what makes most owners agree to look.

The best approach

Business owners say yes when they see that there is no risk. Keep it short, lead with that and the 1,000% Return on Ad Spend, give them the code and invite them to look at the page themselves.

  1. Start with businesses you already know. Places where you are a regular customer, or people you already buy from, are the easiest first conversations. Your 5 purchases during the challenge are a natural way to meet owners.
  2. Be upfront. Say you are a Perk Miner member taking part in the 45-Day Challenge. Honesty builds trust and it is the whole point of the series.
  3. Lead with the risk-free part. They pay only 10% of a sale (capped at $250 per transaction) and only when a sale closes. That is a 1,000% Return on Ad Spend or higher. No sale means $0 in fees.
  4. Give them the code. {{ promo_code }} means zero up-front cost and their first $10 in sales is covered.
  5. Talk to the decision maker. Ask for the owner or manager: "Who handles your advertising?"
  6. Send them your link. Don't try to explain everything yourself. Send your link so they can read it and register.
  7. Follow up once, within 2 or 3 days. A short message is enough.

Answers to common questions

"What does it cost?"
Listing is free. They pay 10% of the total sale, capped at $250 per transaction, and only when a sale is finalized. That is a 1,000% Return on Ad Spend or higher. With code {{ promo_code }} there is zero up-front cost and their first $10 in sales is covered.
"What if my sales are bigger than $10?"
They add funds to their account balance to cover the fee. It is not an advertising fee. It is pre-funded advertising dollars that stay in their balance until they make a sale or close a deal, and unused funds stay in the balance.
"What if I don't get any sales?"
Then there are no fees. No sale means $0.
"Is there a contract?"
No. There are no contracts, commitments, membership fees or hidden fees.
"How do I know a sale came from Perk Miner?"
Members pay the business directly, and the business finalizes each transaction by scanning a QR code (it takes seconds) or remotely. Fees are charged only at that point.
"What do I get besides customers?"
A public business page, real-time metrics, 1% cash back on finalized sales, and referral and mutual commissions.
"Where do I sign up?"
Through your link: {{ business_share_url }}. If they have questions, they can email support@perkminer.com.
Anything you can't answer?
It's fine to say, "Good question, let me find out," and send them to support@perkminer.com.

Do & don't

Do

  • Say you are a Perk Miner member in the 45-Day Challenge.
  • Mention the 1,000% Return on Ad Spend along with the 10% fee, and mention {{ promo_code }}.
  • Explain that pre-funded balance is not an advertising fee.
  • Always send your own link.
  • Accept "no" or "not now" politely and follow up only once.

Don't

  • Promise sales or earnings. The return applies to finalized sales, and results vary.
  • Pressure anyone or keep contacting after a "no".
  • Film anyone without their clear permission.
  • Make up details. If you don't know, point them to support@perkminer.com.
  • Say you are an employee of Perk Miner. You are a member taking part in the challenge.
