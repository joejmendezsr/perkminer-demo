What's the best way to get to $5k?

The best way to get to $5k is to invite others to participate in this journey with you and have them do the challenge themselves. It's possible for you to earn more than the person that sponsored you and at the same time, your direct and in-direct referred members may earn more than you (a member in your 5th tier could reach $5k while you may only get to $1k during the 45-day period). The more people and businesses you invite, the better chances of earning $5k in 45 days (earnings are not based on invites, but from real purchases and real sales).
