How your business earnings work

Total earnings are all cashback and B2B commissions your business has earned, lifetime.

Pending earnings are from invoices finalized in the last 7 days. These are still in the 7-day holding period.

Available for withdrawal are earnings that are at least 7 days old and can be withdrawn to your business bank account.

Each invoice moves from pending to available exactly 7 days after it is finalized as a paid transaction.
