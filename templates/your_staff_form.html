Admins can be regular staff members that need access to all active sessions (restaurant, retail store, etc.) so they can finalize any transaction and, if applicable, message any member.
Service providers can only view, finalize transactions and message members specifically assigned to them. They can also update their status (On the way, etc.), and you, your admin staff members (with permission given) and members (your clients) have the ability to track their live location while "on the way". Service providers also enter the destination address (not admin staff) since they are the ones that physically go to the service address.
