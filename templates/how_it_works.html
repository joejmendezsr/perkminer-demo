How it works for all Perk Miner transactions
Member steps Service request steps Finalization Optional exit
1
Member searches for and visits, or requests service from, a Business of their choice.
Option 1: Member physically visits the advertiser
2A
Member notifies the Business that they're a Perk Miner Member before paying for the purchase.
2B
Member pays the Business directly for their purchase. ONCE PAID, THE TRANSACTION MUST BE FINALIZED (in person or remotely) by the business.
2C
Member clicks "Show QR Code" to display the QR code and activate the finalization process. Member shows the QR Code to the Business, unless the transaction is finalized remotely.
2D
Business Owner or Staff Member is notified that they need to scan a QR code (Member's name is shown) or finalize the transaction remotely.
2E
Both the "Scan Payment QR for (name)" button and the "My Active Sessions" button appear in the Business Dashboard (Business may have to refresh the page for the buttons to appear).
2F1
Business clicks "Scan Payment QR code for (name)" (using any mobile device), scans the QR Code, enters the total paid by Member, then clicks "Finalize".
2F2
To finalize manually (for later finalization if the transaction couldn't be finalized at the time of purchase), the business clicks "My Active Sessions", then the "Finalize Manually" button.
2G
Business ad fee is deducted from Account Balance (Business must have enough in their Account Balance to cover the ad fee).
2H
Both the Member and Business are notified that the transaction has been recorded and they both see their instant cash back.
2I
Member clicks "End session", then the Business ends the session. Both can view earnings in their dashboard.
Option 2: Member requests service with secure messaging
3A
Member clicks "Request Service". A new session begins between the Member and Business (the Member's info is protected).
3B
Member selects the service type, enters a description of the service requested and a budget, then submits the request.
3C
Business is notified by email that their Business has been selected and reviews the service request in their dashboard.
3D
Both the Member and the Business click "My Active Sessions" from their dashboard to view all active requests.
3E
Both select each other to start secure messaging. Member's privacy is protected until Member is ready to share contact info with the business.
3F1
If the Member decides not to proceed, they can "End Session" without sharing any further information (Business does not have access to Member's contact information).
3F2
If the Business decides not to proceed, they can also "End Session" without penalty and without fees (Business does not pay anything for unfinalized leads).
3F3
If the Member decides to continue, they can share address/phone details with the Business (Member decides what information to give).
3G
Business can set appointments or schedule a service call with the Member at no cost, unless the Business charges for service calls. If a Member pays any fees, THE TRANSACTION MUST BE FINALIZED.
3H
Business can send a quote before or after a visit (description of the service and amount), viewable by the Member for that active session only (Member must refresh the page to view the quote).
3I1
If the Member decides not to proceed or accept the terms of the quote, they can "End Session" without penalty. Business may also end the session without costs, unless the Member pays any amount.
3I2
If the Member agrees with the quote, they click "Accept & Pay" (QR appears at the top of the page) and pay the business directly. ONCE PAID, THE TRANSACTION MUST BE FINALIZED BY THE BUSINESS.
3J
Member shows the QR code to the Business so they can scan it. Business can also finalize the transaction remotely.
3K
Business is notified they have a QR code to scan (Business may have to refresh the page).
3L
Business clicks "Scan QR to Finalize Payment" from their active session page (using any mobile device), scans the QR code or finalizes manually, enters the total paid by Member and clicks "Finalize".
3M
Business ad fee is deducted from Account Balance (Business must have enough in their Account Balance to cover the ad fee).
3N
Both the Member and Business are notified that the transaction has been recorded and they both see their instant cash back.
3O
Member clicks "End session", then Business ends the session. Both can view earnings in their dashboard.
