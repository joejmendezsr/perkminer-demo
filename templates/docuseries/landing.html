The majority of the documentation will be self-recorded and uploaded for Perk Miner to use in the docuseries, including first-hand experiences of registering, seeing real earnings in your dashboard and the money getting deposited into your bank account.

At other times, we will be doing live recordings in real business locations (participating members will be informed of the dates, times and locations of these live on-site recordings).

  • If a member chooses to have Perk Miner LLC record a live interaction with a business owner (live invitation), we would love to record the entire interaction, even if they say no, and record your experience.
