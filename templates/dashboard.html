How your earnings work

Total earnings are all cashback and referral commissions you have earned, lifetime.

Pending earnings are from transactions finalized in the last 7 days. These are in a holding period.

Available for withdrawal are earnings that are at least 7 days old and can be withdrawn to your bank account.

Each transaction moves from pending to available exactly 7 days after the business finalizes it.
