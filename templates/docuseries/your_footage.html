How to film and upload your first videos

Hi Participant,

Welcome to the official Perk Miner Challenge Docuseries! We are incredibly excited to document your race to the $5k milestone.

To make sure your footage makes the final cut of our 20-minute premier episode, you must record and upload your first batch of videos within the next 48 hours. Please read and follow these strict production guidelines carefully:

⏱️

Deadline: record and upload your first batch of videos within the next 48 hours.

🚨 3 Golden Rules of Filming

  1. 1
    TURN YOUR PHONE SIDEWAYS: All video must be recorded in Horizontal (Landscape) mode. Vertical videos cannot be used in the broadcast edit.
  2. 2
    LOOK AT THE CAMERA LENS: Do not look at your own face on the screen. Look directly at the physical camera lens on your phone so you are making eye contact with the audience.
  3. 3
    FIND GOOD LIGHTING & QUIET: Stand facing a bright window or record outside during daylight. Make sure all fans, TVs, and background music are turned completely off.

🎥 Your Video Checklist

Total: 60 seconds of footage

Video 1: Intro & Hook 30 seconds

Record yourself from the chest up. State your name, your city, and your raw, honest thoughts about hitting the $5,000 target in 45 days, why you're doing the challenge and what you would do with $5k in 45 days.

e.g., "Hey, I'm Alex from Denver, and I'm either about to make five grand or completely embarrass myself on internet TV and the reason I'm doing the challenge is... $5k would help me pay my college tuition..."

Video 2: The Sign-Up 15 seconds

Record your phone screen (using screen recording or a second phone over your shoulder) as you click 'Register'. Talk out loud about how fast or easy the form is.

Video 3: Connecting Stripe 15 seconds

Record your screen or yourself reacting to linking your Stripe dashboard. Ensure your live balance shows exactly $0.00 on screen so the audience can see your baseline starting point.

📤 How to Submit

Once recorded and you're satisfied with your recording, upload your first two videos (Registration Experience and Intro Experience) files directly to our production server via the link provided in your participant dashboard.

Let's make some history!
